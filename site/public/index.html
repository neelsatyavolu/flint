Read receipts in Flint count only real opens

Each recipient gets their own copy with a private marker. Flint drops the opens it can't trust, so Opened means a person opened it.

  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection shows as Can't confirm, never Opened.
  • Security scanners, bots and your own opens are dropped.
  • Your Sent folder keeps one clean copy.
