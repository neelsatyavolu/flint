The inbox you liked in Spark, without the subscription.

Flint is a Mac mail client for Gmail, Proton and any IMAP account. Its AI runs on the ChatGPT or Grok plan you already pay for. It marks mail as opened only when someone opened it, and your local agents can use it from the command line.

Read receipts

An open means someone opened it.

Most trackers count every image load, so Apple Mail, Outlook's link scanner and your own Sent folder all look like reads. Flint sends each recipient their own copy with a private marker, then throws out the hits it can't trust.

  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection preloads every image, so those recipients show as can't confirm, never as opened.
  • Security scanners and bots that fetch mail on delivery are recognised and dropped.
  • Your own opens, on any of your accounts, don't count.
  • Replies count as the strongest signal of all.

Your Sent folder keeps one clean copy, with no marker in it.

+AI

Bring the AI plan you already have.

Sign in with ChatGPT or Grok once and Flint uses that account. There's no Flint AI tier and nothing extra to pay.

  • Summaries at the top of long threads, with the actions that are waiting on you.
  • Replies and rewrites in your own writing style, plus translation.
  • Ask anything about your mail and get an answer that links to the threads it used.
  • Sorting help for mail the built-in rules aren't sure about.

Nothing is sent to an AI provider until you use an AI feature.

Agents

Agents get a command line. You keep the send button.

The flint command gives local agents the same mailbox you see: list, read, search, draft, archive, snooze and send. Each call shows up in Agent Activity, and mail an agent wants to send waits there until you approve it.

zsh
$ flint search "from:rodney has:attachment"
4821  Rodney Okafor  Freelancing Cycle 3  9:12
$ flint reply 4821 --body "Monday works."
Draft 97 created.
$ flint send 97
Waiting for approval in Flint → Agent Activity…
