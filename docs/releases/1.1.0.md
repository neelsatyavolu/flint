Flint 1.1.0

- Flint has a new look. Backgrounds are a warm off-black, or paper in the light theme, with thin rules in place of boxes and blue only for what is selected or live. Subjects, AI summaries, answers and the Home greeting are set in Newsreader, a reading serif. Times, dates, counts, sizes and labels are set in Geist Mono so they line up as you scan. Corners are smaller, and nothing has shadows or gradients anymore.
- A new update choice: Settings, About, Updates, "Restart into new versions right away". When a new version finishes downloading, Flint saves your drafts, quits and reopens on the new version straight away, even while you're using it. If an update has already downloaded when you pick this choice, Flint restarts at once.
- Flint now checks for updates every hour and when your Mac wakes, instead of every few hours.
- "Read Receipts" and "Agent Activity" are now "Read receipts" and "Agent activity", to match the rest of the app.
