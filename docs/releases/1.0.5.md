Flint 1.0.5

- Gmail app passwords: when Gmail briefly refuses the password, Flint keeps retrying instead of signing the account out. "Sign in again" shows only after several refusals in a row, and goes away by itself if a later try works.
- After your Mac wakes from sleep, every account reconnects right away.
- Opening a conversation: the message you're looking at loads first, and each message downloads once instead of up to three times.
- The summary's placeholder looks like text on the way, the summary fades in when it's written, and the newest message stays where it is while it loads.
- Quick replies don't show placeholders while you skim, and replies that wrap to a second row no longer push the message up.
- New-mail notifications have a Move to Trash button next to Archive and Mark as read.
- Emptying a Notifications or Newsletters bundle takes you back to the Inbox.
