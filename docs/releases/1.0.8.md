Flint 1.0.8

- Logos and wordmarks that set only a height, such as a 22px wordmark in an email header, now show at that size instead of filling the whole message.
- In dark mode, logos on mail that already has its own dark background no longer get a white box behind them.
- Settings > Devices sets up Flint on your iPhone from this Mac. Your accounts, signatures, templates, rules and blocked senders move over your local network, encrypted, and nothing is stored on a server.
