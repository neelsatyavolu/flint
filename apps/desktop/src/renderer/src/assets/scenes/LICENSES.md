# Home scene photos

The landscape photos behind Home. They ship inside the app and are never fetched at runtime. Each one is in the public domain or dedicated to it under CC0, so no attribution is required. We credit the photographers anyway, here and in the credit line on Home.

Every file was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 16:10, scaled to 3200×2000 and encoded as AVIF (quality 47). Works by employees of the US National Park Service (NPS) and the US Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS), made as part of their official duties, are in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105.

`scenes.ts` holds the same credits for the app. `scenes.test.ts` fails when a file here has no manifest entry or no license line.
